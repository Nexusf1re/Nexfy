# Code of Conduct

Nexfy is a small community project. To keep it a good place to work on:

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, in any language.
- No spam, self-promotion or off-topic flooding in issues and pull requests.
- Do not share other people's private information.
- Keep discussions about piracy, bypassing Premium or account sharing out of the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules.

To report a problem privately, use the **Report content** option on the comment or issue in GitHub, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
